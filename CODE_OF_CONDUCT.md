# Community code of conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces must be welcoming, respectful, and safe. Examples of positive
participation include being considerate, accepting constructive feedback,
focusing on what is best for the community, and showing empathy toward other
participants.

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, sustained
disruption, publication of another person's private information, and other
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate are unacceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, reviews, and other
project spaces. It also applies when someone is officially representing the
project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions
should be proportionate, documented privately, and protect the privacy and
safety of people reporting an incident.

Report an incident privately to the repository maintainers. If no dedicated
conduct address is published, use GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting
form and prefix the report title with `[CODE OF CONDUCT]`; do not disclose
sensitive conduct reports in a public issue.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available
at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
